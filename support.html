Common questions

My reminders aren't arriving

Check iPhone Settings → Notifications → Next Up and make sure Allow Notifications is on. A Focus mode (like Sleep or Work) can hide them: add Next Up to the allowed apps for that Focus. Low Power Mode can delay notifications too.

How do I change how reminders sound?

Go to My Time → Reminder style and pick Hype coach, Calm friend or Just the facts. How early reminders come is under My Time → Heads-up.

What is Open time?

The gaps between your blocks show as dashed slots on Today. Tap one to plan something or keep it free on purpose. You can turn it off, change the shortest gap shown, or count it in your week in My Time → Open time.

I'm getting a new phone. How do I move my plan?

On your old phone, open My Time → Backup and tap Save a backup. Save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone (like Files or iCloud Drive). On the new phone, tap Restore on the first setup screen and pick that file.

Can I redo the setup questions?

Yes: My Time → Redo setup. Your blocks stay; routines are replaced with fresh starter routines based on your new answers.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Everything is stored only on your phone, so that removes it completely. There's no account and nothing on any server.

Is Next Up a medical app?

No. Next Up is a planning tool. It doesn't diagnose or treat ADHD or any condition. Please talk to your doctor about your care.

How do I send feedback on the beta?

In TestFlight, take a screenshot inside Next Up and tap Share Beta Feedback, or email us. We read everything.
