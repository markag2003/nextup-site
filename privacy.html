Privacy Policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

The short version: Next Up doesn't collect anything. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you put in the app stays on your phone.

Next Up is an iPhone planner. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What's stored, and where

Your name, your day plan, routines, block types, nudges, wind-down times and settings are saved only on your iPhone, inside the app. We have no servers and never receive any of it.

Health-related things you type

Some nudges or blocks may be about health, like "Take meds". These are just text on your phone. Next Up doesn't read, share or send them anywhere.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone by iOS. Next Up doesn't use push servers, so no reminder content ever leaves your device.

Backups

The app keeps a daily copy on your phone for 7 days. "Save a backup" only shares a file when you choose to, to wherever you pick. It's yours to keep or delete.

Apple, TestFlight and the App Store

If you test a beta through TestFlight or download from the App Store, Apple may collect crash reports and usage data under its own privacy policy, and shares crash reports with us only if you agree. Next Up adds nothing to this. Purchases are handled by Apple; we never see your payment details.

Deleting your data

Delete the app and everything in it is gone for good. There's nothing stored anywhere else to delete.

Ages

Next Up is made for people aged 13 and up.

Your rights

Because we don't hold any of your data, there's nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf. If you have questions about privacy, including under the GDPR or US state privacy laws, email us.

Changes

If Next Up ever starts collecting anything (for example, optional iCloud sync), this policy will be updated first and you'll be asked before it's turned on.

Not medical advice

Next Up is a planning tool. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and it is not a medical device. Talk to your doctor about ADHD care and medication.
